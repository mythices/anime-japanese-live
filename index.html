BEFORE THE LAUNCH

Small app.
A few things to know.

KotobaPlay is being prepared for Windows. Check the supported setup below.

Windows + the right Stremio version

Windows x64 and the WebView2 desktop app at %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Stremio\stremio-shell-ng.exe. Older Qt builds, browser Stremio, Mac and mobile aren't supported.

Japanese subtitles for your video

Use a Japanese track or a matching SRT, VTT or ASS file. The app doesn't supply anime or transcribe audio. Highlighting is an estimated reading guide, not exact speech tracking.

Extra context is optional

Local context uses Ollama + Qwen3 8B. About 7 GB of downloads; allow 15 GB free space. 16 GB RAM or more is recommended. Slower PCs may time out.

QUESTIONS & SUPPORT

A little help, right here.

Launch details and help for existing users.

When can I download the app?

The subscription version is being prepared. Downloads and purchases will open once sign-in and billing are ready.

How much will it cost?

KotobaPlay will require an active $3 USD monthly subscription. Purchases are not open yet.

Do I need an API key?

No API key is needed for local context explanations. They run on your PC and need a separate engine and model download.

I can't see learning subtitles.

Keep the companion open, make sure the toolbar says On, and select a Japanese subtitle track. Some embedded tracks aren't readable: use Load a Japanese subtitle file with a matching file. If the toolbar is missing, reconnect Stremio from the companion.

The pronunciation isn't playing.

Check your internet connection, output device and Stremio's volume. Click once and allow the request to finish; a slow request can take around 45 seconds to time out. Close and reopen the word to retry. The online voice service can be unavailable or rate-limited.

The highlighted word doesn't match the voice.

Use Pace + if the highlight is too slow, or Pace − if it runs ahead. Check that the whole subtitle line is in sync first. The highlight is estimated and can't precisely follow pauses or overlapping voices. You can turn it off.
